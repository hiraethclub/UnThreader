UnThreader

version —

Bulk-deletes your own Threads posts and replies. That's the whole app.

Made because the copycat "delete your Threads" websites want your login and your money to click a button you already own. Deleting your own posts shouldn't cost a subscription. This one runs on your machine, for free, and never sees your password.
